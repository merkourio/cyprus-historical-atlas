# Report C

Scope: eight sites in slice C, every factual claim checked; additions per site; three proposed new sites plus further verified candidates. Maa-Palaeokastro is in the far west (Paphos district), not east/north, but was checked anyway. Wikipedia was used as a pointer and confirmed elsewhere wherever a second source could be reached. Some museum/project pages (British Museum object pages, Princeton department page, Honor Frost Foundation) refused automated fetches; where that matters the verdict says so.

## Site by site

### `enkomi` — Enkomi

#### Verdicts
- CLAIM: "lon 33.88, lat 35.17" — CONFIRMED (35°09′35″N 33°53′18″E). Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Enkomi_(archaeological_site)
- CLAIM: "Active 1900 BCE–1050 BCE" — CONFIRMED with caveat. Earliest material is Middle Bronze Age c. 1900–1700 BCE; the site is thin in the 17th–16th centuries and becomes a real city from c. 1550 BCE; final abandonment c. 1050 BCE. 1900 is defensible; 1650 would show the city rather than the first traces. Sources: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Enkomi_(archaeological_site) ; https://www.livius.org/articles/place/enkomi/ ; https://www.ancientcyprus.com/sites/enkomi
- CLAIM: "very probably the capital of Alashiya" — CORRECTED: "often identified as the capital of Alashiya, though this is debated". Clay analysis of the Alashiya letters points to the southern Troodos foothills; National Geographic notes new research "has called into question Enkomi's status as a capital". Sources: https://www.ancientcyprus.com/sites/enkomi ; https://www.nationalgeographic.com/history/history-magazine/article/enkomi-cyprus-ancient-city-copper-bronze
- CLAIM: "Laid out on a grid, with ashlar buildings, workshops smelting Troodos copper, and a harbour on a lagoon that has since silted up" — CONFIRMED (12th-century rectilinear grid, ashlar buildings, Schaeffer's copper workshops; the Pedieos mouth served as a navigable channel, now silted). Sources: https://www.ancientcyprus.com/sites/enkomi ; https://www.livius.org/articles/place/enkomi/
- CLAIM: "Abandoned after earthquakes around 1050 BCE in favour of Salamis" — CONFIRMED. Sources: https://www.livius.org/articles/place/enkomi/ ; https://www.worldhistory.org/article/1351/ancient-cyprus-a-travel-guide/
- CLAIM: Horned God "55 cm ... found in his sanctuary" — CONFIRMED (54.2 cm, rounded to 0.55 m; found 1948 by Dikaios in a pit in the large ashlar building, early 12th century BCE; Cyprus Museum). Sources: https://cyprusdiscovery.com/enkomi-bronze-figurines-gods-of-copper/ ; https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Enkomi_(archaeological_site)
- CLAIM: Ingot God "bronze warrior standing on an oxhide-shaped copper ingot" — CONFIRMED (c. 35 cm, found 1963 by Schaeffer's French mission in the "Sanctuary of the Ingot God"; Cyprus Museum). Sources: https://www.researchgate.net/figure/ngot-God-statuette-from-enkomi-nicosia-Cyprus-museum-French-mission-1963-no-1616b_fig9_270136615 ; https://www.worldhistory.org/image/10244/bronze-horned-god--ingot-god-from-enkomi-cyprus/
- CLAIM: Cypro-Minoan tablets "c. 1500–1200 BCE ... Now: Cyprus Museum; Louvre" — CORRECTED (date): "c. 1500–1150 BCE". The earliest tablet is c. 1500 BCE; the three fragmentary CM2 tablets are 13th–12th century. Three tablets in the Cyprus Museum, one (AM 2336) in the Louvre; Enkomi is the only Cypriot site with tablets. Sources: https://mnamon.sns.it/index.php?page=Scrittura&id=34&lang=en ; https://mnamon.sns.it/index.php?page=Immagini&id=34&img=580&lang=en ; https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Enkomi_(archaeological_site)
- CLAIM: Mycenaean pictorial kraters "including the so-called Zeus Krater. Dug by the British Museum in 1896. Now: British Museum" — CONFIRMED. The Zeus Krater (robed figure holding scales before a chariot) is early 14th century BCE, so the "14th–13th century" range holds. The British Museum dug c. 100 tombs in 1896 (Turner Bequest), published 1900. Sources: https://www.livius.org/articles/place/enkomi/ ; https://www.britishmuseum.org/collection/term/BIOG60868 ; https://digi.ub.uni-heidelberg.de/diglit/murray1900 . UNCERTAIN: registration number not verified (BM object pages blocked); do not print one.

### `maa` — Maa-Palaeokastro

#### Verdicts
- CLAIM: "lon 32.33, lat 34.85; Coral Bay, Paphos district" — CONFIRMED (headland by Coral Bay, Peyia). Source: https://cyprusdiscovery.com/maa-palaiokastro/
- CLAIM: "Active 1230 BCE–1150 BCE" — CONFIRMED (first occupation c. 1230–1200, destruction and rebuilding c. 1175, abandonment c. 1150 BCE). Source: same.
- CLAIM: "narrow headland with massive walls on both landward and seaward sides" — CONFIRMED. Source: https://www.visitcyprus.com/discover-cyprus/culture/sites-and-monuments/maa-palaeokastro-archaeological-site-and-museum/
- CLAIM: "Founded around 1230 BCE by newcomers with Mycenaean habits, it is the clearest picture of the Greeks' arrival" — CORRECTED: "Founded around 1230 BCE, perhaps by Aegean newcomers, it is one of the sites most often cited for the arrival of Greek settlers on Cyprus". Its material culture is mixed Cypriot/Aegean/Levantine; the "Greek colony" reading is Karageorghis' interpretation. Source: https://www.researchgate.net/publication/333068903_PYLA-KOKKINOKREMOS_AND_MAA-PALAEOKASTRO_A_COMPARISON_OF_TWO_NATURALLY_FORTIFIED_LATE_CYPRIOT_SETTLEMENTS
- CLAIM: Cyclopean fortifications "three metres thick" — CORRECTED: "about 3.5 metres thick and some 70 metres long". Source: https://cyprusdiscovery.com/maa-palaiokastro/
- CLAIM: Mycenaean IIIC pottery "the first produced on the island" — CORRECTED: "among the earliest Greek-style tableware made on the island" (contemporary at Enkomi, Kition, Sinda, Pyla). Source: same ResearchGate comparison.
- CLAIM: "Now: Paphos District Museum; Museum of the Mycenaean Colonisation, Maa" — CONFIRMED (underground museum by Andrea Bruno, 1996, A.G. Leventis Foundation; open weekdays). Sources: https://cyprusdiscovery.com/maa-palaiokastro/ ; https://www.visitcyprus.com/discover-cyprus/culture/sites-and-monuments/maa-palaeokastro-archaeological-site-and-museum/
- Excavation history (for the description): Dikaios 1952; Karageorghis and Demas 1979–1986, published 1988. Source: https://search.worldcat.org/title/excavations-at-maa-palaeokastro-1979-1986/oclc/862245423
